Entry 6.1 · Grades 9 to 11 · Player in Grade 9, Captain in Grades 10 and 11

Junior Varsity Volleyball

Three seasons. Captain for the last two.

The failure in Grade 11 to make varsity was very painful to me. Everybody told me to quit — that continuing junior varsity in Grade 11 was meaningless for schoolwork and for college applications. That was not important to me.

The most valuable thing I have earned from volleyball is not the skills but the people. I still contact the junior captain who introduced me to the high school environment when I was a freshman. I want to be that same kind of person — someone my teammates can reach out to without any burden. The great people I met over three years are worth the time.

Entry 6.2 · Korea Taekwondo Association · Kukkiwon-registered, June 2022

Taekwondo

Fourth grade, registered with the Kukkiwon in June 2022.

Taekwondo is the Korean national martial art, built on kicking, and the sport most Korean children try at some point. I trained from when I was young and graded through to the fourth level.

Grades are separated by mandatory waiting periods that lengthen at each step, so this is not something that can be reached quickly regardless of ability. The years are built into the system.
